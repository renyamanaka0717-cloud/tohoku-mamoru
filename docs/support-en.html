About the App

BrainBox is a timeline-based task management app designed for people with ADHD tendencies or who struggle to keep up with to-do lists. By visualizing today's tasks along a time axis, it helps prevent procrastination.

Timeline view
See your day's tasks laid out along a time axis
Free time visualization
Clearly see the gaps between your plans
Recurring tasks
Set up daily, weekly, and other repeating tasks
Later list
Manage tasks without a set time in one place
Subtasks & notes
Track task details in depth

Reporting a Bug

If you find a bug in the app, please include the following information when contacting our support email.

  • Your device model and OS version
  • The app version (available in Settings)
  • What happened and how to reproduce it
  • A screenshot, if possible
