About BrainBox

BrainBox (the "App") is a timeline-style task management app that visualizes your day's tasks along a time axis, designed for people with ADHD tendencies or who struggle to keep up with to-do lists.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled in this App.

Information We Collect

This App stores the following information only on your device.

  • Input data such as task names, dates/times, memos, and subtasks
  • Settings such as wake and sleep times
  • Customization information such as tags, categories, and recurrence settings

This information is never sent to external servers and is managed only on your device.

Purpose of Use

The information collected is used only for the following purposes.

  • Providing task display, management, and search features
  • Generating schedules for recurring tasks
  • Retaining app settings

Third-Party Disclosure

This App does not provide your personal information to any third party.

However, if you use the optional AI text generation feature (which uses the Groq API), the task information you enter may be sent to that API. Please review Groq's privacy policy for details.

Data Management

All data for this App is stored on your device (localStorage).

  • All data is deleted when you uninstall the App
  • Data may be lost if your device is reset
  • This App has no cloud backup feature for data
