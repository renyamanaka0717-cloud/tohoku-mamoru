Delete Account & Data
For users who have signed in with Google or Apple

Who this applies to

BrainBox stores your data locally on your device by default and does not send it to any server.

If you have signed in via the Settings screen (e.g. with Google), your tasks and settings are also saved to the cloud (Firebase/Cloud Firestore) for backup and multi-device sync. This page explains how to delete that account and its cloud data.

Delete from within the app (recommended)

This is the fastest and most reliable method, if you still have access to the app.

  1. Open the BrainBox app
  2. Go to Settings → Account
  3. Tap "Delete Account" near the bottom of the screen
  4. Review the details and tap "Delete Account" again to confirm

Your account and the cloud data listed below will be permanently deleted immediately.

What gets deleted vs. what stays

Deleting your account permanently removes the following cloud data. Deleted

  • Tasks, shopping list, file tabs, and tags
  • Notification settings (time-based, location-based, and don't-forget alerts)
  • Life patterns, move history, and bulk-input history
  • App settings (wake/sleep times, theme color, etc.)
  • Your sign-in account itself (email and other auth credentials)

Data stored locally on your device is not deleted when you delete your account. BrainBox continues to work fully using only local data when you're signed out. If you'd also like to remove the data on your device, simply uninstall the app.

If you can't access the app

If you've switched devices or otherwise can't access the app, please contact us using one of the methods below, including the email address you signed in with. We'll verify your identity and delete your data within 3–5 business days.
