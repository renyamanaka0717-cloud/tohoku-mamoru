About the App

BrainBox is a timeline-based task management app designed for people with ADHD tendencies or who struggle to keep up with to-do lists. By visualizing today's tasks along a time axis, it helps prevent procrastination.

FAQ

Q. Where is my data stored?
A. All data is stored in local storage on your device. Nothing is sent to external servers.
Q. What happens to my data if I delete the app?
A. All data is deleted when you uninstall the app. There is currently no cloud backup feature.
Q. I accidentally deleted a task. Can I restore it?
A. Sorry, there is currently no way to restore a deleted task. Please double-check before deleting important tasks.
Q. Can I delete just one occurrence of a recurring task?
A. Yes. When deleting a recurring task, you can choose "Delete this occurrence only" or "Delete all occurrences."
Q. Where do I subscribe to or cancel the PRO plan?
A. You can subscribe from PRO in the app's Settings. You can cancel any time from your Apple ID account settings (Subscriptions) in your device's Settings app.
Q. Where can I see tasks moved to "Later"?
A. Tap the "Later" button on the bottom bar to see the Later list.
